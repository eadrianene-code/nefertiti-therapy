Questions clients ask most

How much does a massage cost in Brașov at Nefertiti Therapy?

60-minute sessions start at 200 lei. Deep sports massage and Supreme Power cost 250 lei, Deep Relaxing is 225 lei for 70 minutes, and the 90-minute packages (Supreme: Rebirth Package and Bedouin Massage) cost 300 lei. The 180-minute 7 Chakra session costs 600 lei.

Do you offer couples massage, and how much is it?

Yes, it's our most requested service. Two therapists work at the same time in the same room, so two people are treated together, each with the massage they prefer. It costs 200 lei per person for 60 minutes of relaxation or therapeutic massage, so 400 lei in total for two.

Do you speak English?

Yes. You can book in English by phone at +40 770 692 621, and the therapist will ask about pressure and problem areas before starting.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

We recommend booking by phone at +40 770 692 621, as we work in fixed slots and are often full. If we have a free slot that day, we can also take walk-ins.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 11:00 to 21:00, including Saturday and Sunday. The last 60-minute session starts at 20:00.

Where are you located?

Strada Codrii Cosminului 40, Brașov, Romania. There is street parking in front of the building.

What kind of massage do you offer?

Nefertiti Therapy is a professional massage studio. We offer only relaxation, therapeutic and sports massage and reflexology by trained therapists. We do not offer sexual services of any kind, and such requests are refused.

Which massage should I choose for back or neck pain?

The 60-minute therapeutic massage, because it works precisely on knots. If you also want relaxation, choose the Combined Massage. For old tension and strong stiffness, we recommend Supreme Power.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Revolut and bank transfer. Gift vouchers can be bought at the studio or paid online by Revolut or bank transfer.

It's my first massage. What should I expect?

Arrive 5 minutes early. The therapist asks what hurts and what pressure you prefer, then leaves you alone in the room to get ready. You are covered with a towel for the whole session and only the area being worked on is uncovered. You can ask for more or less pressure at any time.
