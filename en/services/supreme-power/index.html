Supreme Power is the strongest 60-minute massage we do. The therapist works with forearms and elbows, with high pressure and a steady rhythm, on the back, shoulders, neck and legs. It isn't a session to fall asleep in. It's for people who find regular massages too soft.

How we work

  • Forearms and elbows, not just palms, so the pressure reaches the deep layers of the muscle.
  • A steady rhythm on the large groups: back, shoulders, neck and legs.
  • Extra time on old knots, the ones you have felt for months.
  • You control the intensity. Say harder or softer and the therapist adjusts at once.

Who it is for

  • Your muscles are very tight and your back feels like a block.
  • You've had many massages and they felt too light.
  • You do physical work or train hard and want deep pressure.
  • You are looking for what other places call a deep tissue massage.

Who it is not for

If this is your first massage, or you mainly want to relax, start with the relaxation massage or the Combined Massage. If your tension comes from training, the deep sports massage also includes assisted stretches.

Afterwards

You may feel mild muscle soreness the next day, as after a workout. It is normal and passes quickly. Drink water and avoid hard effort on the day of the session.
