In pregnancy, weight is redistributed and the body pays for it in the lower back, hips and legs. A prenatal massage doesn't change the pregnancy, but it can make the weeks much more comfortable.

What we work on

  • Lower back, which carries most of the extra weight.
  • Hips, where the constant feeling of stretching appears.
  • Legs and ankles, for the heavy feeling at the end of the day.
  • Shoulders, neck and scalp, for the tension everyone collects there.

The rules we follow

  • You lie on your side, with support pillows under the belly and between the knees. Never on your belly.
  • Pressure stays gentle throughout the session.
  • We avoid deep techniques on the abdomen and the low lumbar area.
  • You tell us how many weeks pregnant you are and whether your doctor has given any restrictions.

When to come, and when to ask your doctor first

Most mothers-to-be come in the second and third trimester. In the first trimester many prefer to wait, and that is a decision you make with your doctor, not with us. If you have a high-risk pregnancy, high blood pressure, pre-eclampsia, bleeding or any other complication, we need your doctor's agreement before booking you.

The oils we use

We use 100% natural oils with no synthetic fragrance. If you are sensitive to smells, tell us when booking and we prepare a neutral oil.
