At Nefertiti Therapy, a couples massage doesn't mean two sessions one after the other. It means two therapists working at the same time, in the same room. Two people are treated together, each with the massage that suits them.

What makes it different

  • Two therapists at once. No taking turns; you are both treated in the same session.
  • Each person chooses. Relaxation, therapeutic or sports massage, depending on what each of you needs.
  • Professional standard. Trained therapists, 100% natural oils, hot stones and a clean, quiet room.
  • For any two people. Partners, friends, siblings, or a parent and child travelling together.
  • English spoken. Book by phone in English; your therapists will ask about pressure and problem areas.

How a session works

  1. Call us with the day and time. We need two therapists free in the same slot, so booking ahead helps.
  2. When you arrive, each of you says which massage you want and what pressure you prefer. You don't have to choose the same thing.
  3. You go into the same room, on two tables side by side. Calm music plays and each of you is covered with a towel throughout.
  4. You are both massaged at the same time, each by your own therapist, for 60 or 90 minutes depending on what you chose.

How much a couples massage costs

You pay per person, depending on what each of you chooses. For relaxation or therapeutic massage the price is 200 lei per person for 60 minutes, so 400 lei in total for two.

If one of you wants something else, only the difference is added for that person. Deep sports massage is 250 lei, and the 90-minute packages are 300 lei. See all massages and prices.
