Published 15 November 2026 · Nefertiti Therapy, Brașov

In many places, a couples massage means one of you waits while the other is on the table. With us, two therapists work at the same time, in the same room: you are both on the table together. It is our most requested service.

How it works

  1. Call +40 770 692 621 and say there are two of you. Two therapists need to be free in the same slot, so booking ahead matters.
  2. At the start, each of you says what hurts and what pressure you prefer.
  3. Each of you chooses a massage: relaxation, therapeutic or sports. You don't have to choose the same one.
  4. The session lasts 60 minutes, for both of you at the same time.

What it costs

200 lei per person for 60 minutes of relaxation or therapeutic massage, so 400 lei in total for two. If one of you chooses something else, such as sports massage, only the difference is added.

Not only for couples

Partners come, but so do friends, siblings, or a parent and child travelling together. The idea is simple: two people who want to come together and not take turns.

On a trip to Brașov

A couples massage fits well at the end of a day on the mountain or walking the old town. We are open every day until 21:00, and the last 60-minute session starts at 20:00. Payment is by cash, Revolut or bank transfer.
