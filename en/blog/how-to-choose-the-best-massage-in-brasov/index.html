What to check at any massage studio

  • Published prices. A serious studio tells you the price before you arrive.
  • Many reviews, not just good ones. Ten five-star reviews say less than two hundred with a 4.7 average. Read the critical ones too.
  • Clear descriptions. Which areas are worked, how long it lasts and what pressure is used.
  • Questions at the start. A good therapist asks what hurts and what pressure you prefer before beginning.
  • Clear address and hours. You know where you are going and how late they work.
