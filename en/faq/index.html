How much does a massage cost in Brașov at Nefertiti Therapy?

60-minute sessions start at 200 lei. Deep sports massage and Supreme Power cost 250 lei, Deep Relaxing is 250 lei for 75 minutes, and the 90-minute packages (Supreme: Rebirth Package and Bedouin Massage) cost 300 lei. The 180-minute 7 Chakra session costs 600 lei. See all massages and prices.

Do you speak English?

Yes. You can book in English by phone at +40 770 692 621, and the therapist will ask about pressure and problem areas before starting.

Do you offer couples massage, and how much is it?

Yes, it's our most requested service. Two therapists work at the same time in the same room, so two people are treated together, each with the massage they prefer. It costs 200 lei per person for 60 minutes of relaxation or therapeutic massage, so 400 lei in total for two. See the couples massage page.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

We recommend booking by phone at +40 770 692 621, as we work in fixed slots and are often full. If we have a free slot that day, we can also take walk-ins.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 11:00 to 21:00, including Saturday and Sunday. The last 60-minute session starts at 20:00.

Where are you located?

Strada Codrul Cosminului 40, Brașov, Romania. There is street parking in front of the building.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Revolut and bank transfer. Payment is made at the end of the session. Gift vouchers can be bought at the studio or paid online by Revolut or bank transfer.

What kind of massage do you offer?

Nefertiti Therapy is a professional massage studio. We offer only relaxation, therapeutic and sports massage and reflexology, given by trained therapists.

Which massage should I choose for back or neck pain?

The 60-minute therapeutic massage, because it works precisely on knots. If you also want relaxation, choose the Combined Massage. For old tension and strong stiffness, we recommend Supreme Power.

Which massage is best after hiking or skiing?

The deep sports massage for tired legs and stiff muscles, or reflexology if it is mainly your feet. If you simply want to warm up and rest, choose Deep Relaxing, with three kinds of hot stones.

Do you have hot stone massage?

Yes. Hot stones are included in our sessions at no extra cost. Deep Relaxing and the Supreme: Rebirth Package use three kinds of hot stones.

It's my first massage. What should I expect?

Arrive 5 minutes early. The therapist asks what hurts and what pressure you prefer, then leaves you alone in the room to get ready. You are covered with a towel for the whole session and only the area being worked on is uncovered. You can ask for more or less pressure at any time.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes. Any session can be bought as a personalised gift voucher, at the studio or paid online by Revolut or bank transfer.
